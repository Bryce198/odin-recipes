Spaghetti

The Ingredients

For this easy spaghetti recipe, you will need:

  • a box of Barilla Pronto Half-Cut Spaghetti noodles or your favorite pasta
  • ground beef or sirloin – you can also swap ground turkey, ground chicken, ground Italian sausage or another ground protein of your choice
  • a jar of your favorite tomato sauce or marinara sauce, or homemade spaghetti sauce if you have it on hand
  • fresh basil for garnishing
  • freshly grated Parmesan cheese for serving if desired
  • Salt, black pepper, red pepper flakes, oregano, parsley or other seasonings or herbs are optional and you can add them to taste

The Recipe:

  1. To make this spaghetti recipe with ground beef, first cook the pasta according to package instructions in a large pot or Dutch oven.
  2. Then brown the ground beef over medium-high heat, breaking it up with a spatula as it cooks. No need to drain the grease if you use lean ground beef.
  3. After beef has cooked through, add the pasta sauce and stir to combine and bring it to a simmer. Continue cooking until the meat sauce is completely heated through.
  4. After the pasta has finished cooking, plate the pasta and add sauce to each individual plate as desired. I garnished my homemade spaghetti with fresh basil and Parmesan cheese, but feel free to omit if you don’t have either on hand.
